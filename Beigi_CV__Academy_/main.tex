\section{RESEARCH INTERESTS}

My research interests focus on traffic flow theory, behavior modeling, CAVs, simulation, machine learning, and computer vision to enhance safety, efficiency, and resilience in transportation systems:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence:} Developing deep reinforcement learning architectures, including multi-agent reinforcement learning, foundation models, vision-language models, diffusion models, and imitation learning for autonomous driving applications.
    
    \item \textbf{Automated Driving Systems (ADS) and Trajectory Planning:} Designing perception, prediction, and planning pipelines for autonomous vehicles using multi-sensor fusion. Focus areas include generating trajectory models for ADAS and enhancing situational awareness in mixed-traffic environments.
    
    \item \textbf{Traffic Flow Theory and Traffic Simulation:} Macroscopic and microscopic traffic flow analysis, integrating data-driven and physics-based behavior models into large-scale microsimulation tools. Building integrated simulation pipelines for closed-loop decision-making using real-time data streaming.
    
    \item \textbf{Computer Vision and Large-Scale Data Modeling:} Engineering computer vision pipelines to extract large-scale trajectory data for AV-human interaction modeling and motion prediction. Utilizing multi-modal deep learning time-series models to monitor driver behavior and deploy real-time IoT alerts.
    
    \item \textbf{Transportation Operations and Safety Optimization:} Applying Bayesian optimization, genetic algorithms, and reinforcement learning to calibrate traffic models and evaluate vulnerable road user behavior. Analyzing the impact of V2X connectivity on intersection performance and overall network reliability.
\end{itemize}
